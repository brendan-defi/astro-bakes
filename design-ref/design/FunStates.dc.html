Meet Astro

Astro is our border collie, and the name on the box. He watches every bake from the kitchen floor and has never once been allowed a cookie.

We handle the baking: the stuff you'd find on an American kitchen counter. Thick cookies with gooey middles, banana bread, chocolate cake.

It's all made in small batches in Nakameguro, one drop at a time, so nothing sits on a shelf waiting for you.
